# 🌌 Research Paper: Earth-Mars Orbital Dynamics & Radio Latency

## 1. Physical Principles
The speed of light ($c = 299,792.458 \text{ km/s}$) dictates the time delay of electromagnetic transmissions across interplanetary distances.

$$t_{\text{delay}} = \frac{d_{\text{orbit}}}{c}$$

## 2. Orbital Configurations
- **Opposition (Closest Approach)**: $d \approx 54.6 \times 10^6 \text{ km} \implies t \approx 182 \text{ seconds } (\approx 3.0 \text{ minutes})$.
- **Conjunction (Max Distance)**: $d \approx 401 \times 10^6 \text{ km} \implies t \approx 1337 \text{ seconds } (\approx 22.3 \text{ minutes})$.
- **Solar Conjunction Blackout**: Occurs when Mars moves directly behind the Sun relative to Earth. The solar corona disrupts radio frequencies, causing complete communications blackout lasting up to two weeks.
